# ksheera_sagara
Ksheera-Sagara is a specialized Android application designed to empower dairy farmers by digitizing their financial and operational records. The app features a secure authentication system and a localized interface supporting English, Hindi, and Kannada to ensure ease of use across regional demographics. At its core, the dashboard allows farmers to effortlessly log daily milk slips and meticulously track overhead costs—including fodder, medicine, and labor wages—which are then automatically processed to calculate daily and weekly profit margins. By leveraging a Room database for historical storage and providing visual analytics, the app replaces traditional paper ledgers with a robust, offline-capable tool that gives farmers a clear, real-time understanding of their dairy business's net income.
